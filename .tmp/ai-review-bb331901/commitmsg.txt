Fix residual double-count and bucket-index severance found in verification

Verification pass (opus + pentester) on the round-3 fixes. Pentester APPROVED
both its MAJORs closed, with the version gate re-executed across a full
accept/refuse matrix on 4.7.stable (no false accept; floor(INF)==INF is true, so
the range clause is load-bearing). Opus closed 9 of 10 and withdrew its TYPE_INT
claim after verifying the engine agrees with the plan. Two regressions found:

- CRITICAL: the round-3 watermark rule carried the sub-minute residual twice --
  sim_wall_time = now - residual leaves the watermark behind by the residual, so
  the next elapsed already contains it and total = residual + elapsed adds it
  again. Reproduced independently: 120 cycles of 29s credited 119 steps instead
  of 58 (2.05x), and 3600 cycles of 1s credited 899 instead of 60 (15x). Fixed
  by committing sim_wall_time = now and carrying the residual in one place only;
  verified exactly conservative at 29s, 1s and 60s cycles.
- MAJOR: redefining sim_wall_time as an epoch instant severed the identity that
  gave catch-up its curve-bucket index (round 3 had it as ticks x 0.05, so the
  index was sim_wall_time/60 by definition). Catch-up now advances the sim-minute
  index by one per step, and that index -- not the watermark -- selects the
  bucket. Test A's bound needs both paths indexing the same buckets.
- §8.3 now states that clamp-discarded time is forfeited (sim_wall_time snaps to
  now), resolving a contradiction where the doc asserted both that nothing is
  surrendered to catch-up and that stall lag is credited at the next boundary.
  Forfeiting keeps elapsed meaning hidden duration only, so accrued lag cannot
  trip the reconciliation threshold on a three-second app-switch.
- offline_efficiency's binding constraint written down (min over the Test B
  matrix of lived/ceiling, not merely "below 1") and added to §13.
- §10.1 now requires restored:yes after phone-lock plus app-switcher kill --
  the only on-device check that the synchronous hidden-save actually landed.
- Corrected drift constant to the measured 1.27e-12; deep nesting restored to
  the hostile-input matrix as a guard on engine behaviour the design relies on;
  shaft cap stated as 8 with the gutter budget; tests renumbered 1-18 with the
  cap test moved to the Milestone-1 block.
